**RSRequestify: Reforged** is an addon for **[<span style="color: #3366ff;">Refined Storage</span>](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/refined-storage)** that adds the Requester and Crafting Emitter from RS:Requestify to your Refined Storage in Minecraft 26.1+!

 

**Requester:** Keeps a specified amount of an item/fluid in stock in your Refined Storage.

**Crafting Emitter:** Emits a Redstone Signal when the specified amount of an item/fluid is being processed.

 

[**<span style="color: #3366ff;">Originally made by Buuz135</span>**](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/rs-requestify), rewritten to work on Minecraft 26.1+ by me.
